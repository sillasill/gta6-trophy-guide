Sources & honesty

Grand Theft Auto VI released on 19 November 2026 — the day this guide was written. No guide can have verified every step in the game yet, so this page is honest about what is confirmed and what is not.

What we know, and what we do not

  • Confirmed by two lists: every trophy's name, description and grade, as Exophase and PSNProfiles list them — the two agree on all 55. PSN's progress counts (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone race locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas, and the rest) come from PSNProfiles.
  • Reported by the early guides: the tips in each trophy's guide, credited to GTABase and PowerPyx as reported by EGamers. Where only one source gives a tip, the guide says so.
  • Not yet verified: five trophies have no confirmed unlock method at launch — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin — plus the animal for Fair Game. Their guides say so plainly instead of inventing steps.
  • Possibly missable: one trophy, Positive Mental Attitude, may depend on a Chapter 1 meditation. The guides that flag it call it unconfirmed, and this guide repeats that caution word for word.
  • No online trophies: all 55 are single-player and offline. Cheats are reported to disable trophy tracking and saving, so leave them off for a trophy run.
