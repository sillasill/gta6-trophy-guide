    There is no mission replay

    GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so a story mission cannot be played again once it is done. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission, and rotate the rest. Everything outside the story stays available after the credits. Source: Output Lag — GTA 6 Trophy List (first-day guide, 18–19 Nov 2026).
